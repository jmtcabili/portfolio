During my internship at American International Assurance, I built an automated data extraction and preprocessing pipeline with Python, Pandas, SQL and Azure Databricks.

The pipeline curated a dataset of 6.9k+ emails. It filtered out personally identifiable information and attached LLM-generated labels, so the dataset could be used downstream for email intent classification.
